Our Kitchen Story • Founded in Indiranagar

Mexican Fire.
Bangalore Soul.

Kaazos was born out of an obsession with real flavor. We asked a simple question: why does convenient, quick-casual dining so often rely on frozen pre-mixes, bottled dressings, and artificial seasonings? When did real cooking get replaced by opening commercial factory bags?

We believed Bengaluru deserved better. Every single morning at 7:00 AM in our Indiranagar kitchen on 100ft Road, our team chops fresh farm tomatoes, roasts whole dried chillies over an open flame, slow-simmers black beans, and marinates tender chicken in citrus and house-ground spices.

We bring the raw, rustic authenticity of traditional Mexican street taquerías and fuse it with the bold punch and craveable warmth that Indian palates adore. No shortcuts, zero preservatives — just honest food you can eat every day and feel great about.

A Day in the Kitchen

Real food takes patience and craft. Here is how our team crafts your meal from dawn to evening service.

07:00 AM

Farm Harvest Arrival

Fresh tomatoes, crisp cilantro, limes, and farm-raised poultry arrive at our back door from nearby Karnataka farms.

08:30 AM

Scratch Prep & Salsas

Chefs roast whole peppers, hand-dice crisp onions for pico de gallo, and start the 3-hour simmer on black beans.

10:30 AM

Marinades & Grill Fire

Cast iron planchas heat up. Chicken breasts marinate in house adobo, while warm tortilla chips get their first golden fry.

11:00 AM

Doors Open For You

Open kitchen service begins. You pick your base, watch each component get layered, and enjoy food cooked with honest passion.

Scratch-cooked Mexican street cuisine fused with bold Indian spices. Pure ingredients, zero preservatives, and honest craftsmanship.
