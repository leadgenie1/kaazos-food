Our Story

Mexican Fire.
Bangalore Soul.

Kaazos was born out of an obsession with real flavor. We asked a simple question: why does convenient, quick-casual dining so often rely on frozen pre-mixes, bottled dressings, and artificial seasonings?

We believed Bengaluru deserved better. Every single morning at 7:00 AM in our Indiranagar kitchen, our team chops fresh farm tomatoes, roasts whole dried chillies over an open flame, slow-simmers black beans, and marinates tender chicken in citrus and house-ground spices.

We bring the raw, rustic authenticity of traditional Mexican street taquerías and fuse it with the bold punch and craveable warmth that Indian palates adore. No shortcuts, zero preservatives — just honest food you can eat every day and feel great about.
